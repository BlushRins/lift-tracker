Questions

Does it cost anything?

No. It’s free, with no ads and no sign-up.

Where is my log stored?

On your phone, in the browser’s storage for the app. Clearing the site’s data would remove it, so export a backup now and then.

Does it work without signal?

Yes, once you’ve opened it with internet one time.

How do I put it on my home screen?

iPhone: open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: open it in Chrome and choose Install app.

Can I log pull-ups and dips?

Yes. They log as BW, or BW plus added weight, like BW +10 kg.

What counts as a PR?

Your heaviest weight for that lift. At the same weight, more reps counts too.

Can I move my log to a new phone?

Yes. Export a backup on the old phone and import it on the new one.

Is there a pounds mode?

Not yet. Everything is in kilograms.
