How it's scored

Your best set is the heaviest weight you lifted. If two sets used the same weight, the one with more reps wins.

  • 80 kg × 8, then 80 kg × 10 next week → +2 reps, a PR
  • 80 kg × 8, then 85 kg × 8 → +5 kg, a PR

Strength gained adds up how many kg heavier your best set is now than in your first week, across all your lifts.
