How strength is scored

Every set gets one strength score, its estimated one-rep max (e1RM): the most you could probably lift once.

e1RM = kg × (1 + reps ÷ 30)
  • 80 kg × 8 → 101.3
  • 80 kg × 10 the next week → 106.7, so +5.3 kg stronger
  • 85 kg × 8 instead → 107.7, so +6.3 kg stronger

Each week counts your best set per lift. A PR is a set that beats your best ever for that lift.
